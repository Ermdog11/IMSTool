# CoPublisher AI — promo video script

**For:** prospective partners and investors
**Length:** about 3:00 (full cut), plus a 60-second cut
**Mockups:** `mockups/01…18.png`. They are real screens from the app, filled with **sample data**: the players, outlets, headlines and numbers are invented. Keep the on-screen "Sample data" tag on any scene that shows product numbers.
**Industry figures** in Scene 1 come from published research (sources at the bottom). Check each against its source before you publish the video.

---

## Directions for the AI video tool

Paste this block into the tool's style or prompt box:

> Modern SaaS product video. Clean, confident, fast-paced but not frantic. Dark navy (#0f1b2d) and bright blue (#2563eb) brand colors, white type, a geometric sans-serif font. Screens are shown as crisp product screenshots inside a thin browser frame (or a phone frame where noted), with slow push-ins and pans toward the area being described, and soft blue highlight boxes around key UI elements. Subtle motion graphics for numbers and labels. Upbeat, modern instrumental music: driving, newsroom energy, low under the voiceover. Voiceover: warm, credible, conversational American voice, mid-30s to 40s; sounds like an editor, not an announcer. Add a small "Sample data" tag in the lower-left corner whenever a product screen shows numbers.

**Pacing:** about 150 words per minute. Each scene's voiceover fits its time.

Every story you're late on is readers, subscribers and ad revenue handed to someone else. That's why nearly every publisher now calls automation a priority.

## Accuracy notes

These are the things the script says the product does, matched to what it actually does today:

- **"Working 24 hours a day":** scheduled jobs run all day and night. X is checked every 30 minutes, competitors every 15 minutes, hot stories every 15 minutes, and full scans several times a day. The morning memo goes out at 7 a.m.
- **"Sends it straight to your phone":** breaking and major stories (rated 4–5) are auto-drafted and emailed with an "Open in Content Editor" button. They also drop into Team Chat. Texts aren't available yet.
- **Social platforms:** Facebook, Instagram and Threads numbers cover posts sent through Buffer; X covers every tweet on your own account.
- **"Your newsroom's voice":** breaking drafts follow your house style guide; the Content Editor also matches each writer's own voice when a writer profile is set up.
- **Search:** Google Search Console needs access to a property that covers your article pages.
