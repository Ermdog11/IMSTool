# CoPublisher AI — promo video script

**For:** prospective partners and investors
**Length:** about 2:30 (full cut), plus a 60-second cut at the end
**Mockups:** `mockups/01…14.png`. They are real screens from the app, filled with **sample data**: the players, outlets, headlines and numbers are invented. Keep the on-screen "Sample data" tag on any scene that shows numbers.

---

## Directions for the AI video tool

Paste this block into the tool's style or prompt box:

> Modern SaaS product video. Clean, confident, fast-paced but not frantic. Dark navy (#0f1b2d) and bright blue (#2563eb) brand colors, white type, a geometric sans-serif font. Screens are shown as crisp product screenshots inside a thin browser frame, with slow push-ins and pans toward the area being described, and soft blue highlight boxes around key UI elements. Subtle motion graphics for numbers and labels. Upbeat, modern instrumental music: driving, newsroom energy, low under the voiceover. Voiceover: warm, credible, conversational American voice, mid-30s to 40s; sounds like an editor, not an announcer. Add a small "Sample data" tag in the lower-left corner whenever a screen shows numbers.

**Pacing:** about 150 words per minute. Each scene's voiceover fits its time.
**Highlight boxes:** each scene names the exact UI element to circle or zoom into.

## Full cut (about 2:30)

### Scene 1 — The problem (0:00–0:12)
- **Visual:** No product yet. Quick cuts of a cluttered screen: dozens of browser tabs, a phone buzzing with notifications, a clock ticking past midnight. Generate this as stock-style footage, or use plain motion graphics: tab icons multiplying.
- **On-screen text:** "Your beat never sleeps."
- **Voiceover:**
  > Every beat publisher knows the feeling. A commitment drops at 11 p.m. A rival posts first. And somewhere in sixty open tabs was the story you needed.

### Scene 2 — Introduce CoPublisher (0:12–0:20)
- **Visual:** CoPublisher AI logo on navy, then a fast pull-back revealing `01-news-feed.png` in a browser frame.
- **On-screen text:** "CoPublisher AI — the AI newsroom for beat publishers"
- **Voiceover:**
  > CoPublisher AI is an assistant editor that never clocks out. It watches your entire beat, tells you what matters, and helps you publish it first.

### Scene 3 — Every story on your beat, rated (0:20–0:38)
- **Visual:** `01-news-feed.png`. Slow push-in on the top two cards. Highlight boxes on the red **"5 — Breaking"** badges, then the **"Watched account"** tag, then the row of filter chips.
- **On-screen text:** "Dozens of sources · every story rated 1–5 · breaking news first"
- **Voiceover:**
  > It scans news sites, Google News, Reddit, podcasts, YouTube, and the reporters you trust on X, all day long. Every story is rated one to five, so the commitment and the transfer jump to the top and the noise falls away. Old highlight clips and recycled stories get filtered out before you ever see them.

### Scene 4 — From alert to article in one click (0:38–0:55)
- **Visual:** `02-write-it-card.png` full frame. Highlight the **"Write it"** button and the "Draft ready" line. Hard cut to `08-content-editor.png`; pan slowly down from the headline options to the edited article.
- **On-screen text:** "One click: alert → publish-ready draft"
- **Voiceover:**
  > See a story you want? Click Write it. Seconds later there's a draft in your house style, with links to your own past coverage and three headline options, from straight news to SEO-first. Your writer reviews it and hits send.

### Scene 5 — An editor that sounds like your writers (0:55–1:10)
- **Visual:** `09-content-editor-notes-seo.png`. Highlight **Editor's notes**, then **Context added**, with the yellow [VERIFY] callout zoomed in.
- **On-screen text:** "Your voice. Your style. Facts flagged."
- **Voiceover:**
  > The Content Editor copyedits every draft in your house style while keeping each writer's voice. It adds context, writes the SEO, and flags anything that needs checking, so speed never costs you accuracy.

### Scene 6 — Know what the competition just published (1:10–1:25)
- **Visual:** `07-opps-spy.png`. Push in on the three competitor cards; highlight the red **"3 gaps"**. Then pan down the list; highlight the red **"Gap"** labels against the green **"We covered it"**.
- **On-screen text:** "Opps Spy: every competitor story, within 15 minutes"
- **Voiceover:**
  > Opps Spy follows your closest competitors. Every story they publish on your beat shows up within fifteen minutes, flagged as a gap if you haven't covered it. You can even get an email the moment they post, with texts coming soon.

### Scene 7 — An assistant editor's memo, every morning (1:25–1:45)
- **Visual:** `14-coverage-desk-email.png` shown inside a phone or email-client frame. Scroll slowly from the "Yesterday at a glance" box down to "Today's priorities". Highlight the orange **"no social posts"** flag on the depth chart story.
- **On-screen text:** "Every morning at 7: what worked, what's next"
- **Voiceover:**
  > Every morning at seven, the Coverage Desk lands in your inbox. It covers yesterday's traffic, your social numbers and where readers came from, along with today's priorities and the gaps to fill. It even catches the story that drew fourteen hundred readers but never got shared.

### Scene 8 — Analytics that tell you what to do (1:45–2:05)
- **Visual:** `06-trends-charts.png`. Start on the **Suggestions** list and highlight each title as it's read. Then pan down to the **"Where readers came from"** bars and the **8 pm** bar.
  Quick cut to `04-x-analytics.png` with the **Last 7 days / Last 30 days** tabs highlighted.
- **On-screen text:** "Chartbeat · X · Buffer · Search Console — in one place"
- **Voiceover:**
  > Connect Chartbeat, X, Buffer and Google Search Console, and CoPublisher turns the numbers into decisions. Publish by seven-thirty. Re-share the depth chart. Lean into search. Plain-English suggestions, each backed by your own data.

### Scene 9 — Set up in minutes (2:05–2:20)
- **Visual:** Quick sequence, about 4 seconds each: `10-wizard-1-outlet.png` → `11-wizard-2-beat-built.png` (highlight the **"Build my beat"** button and the line "Added 5 sources, 2 podcasts and 2 YouTube channels") → `12-wizard-3-sources-podcasts.png` → `13-wizard-4-done.png` (highlight **"You're all set"**).
- **On-screen text:** "Name your beat. CoPublisher builds the rest."
- **Voiceover:**
  > Getting started takes minutes. Name your team, and CoPublisher builds your beat for you: the sources, the podcasts, the YouTube channels and the names that matter. Rate them, and you're live.

### Scene 10 — Close (2:20–2:30)
- **Visual:** The news feed (`01-news-feed.png`) pulls back into a grid of all the screens, then cuts to the logo on navy.
- **On-screen text:** "CoPublisher AI · Be first. Be right. Be everywhere." + contact line: `[your email / website]`
- **Voiceover:**
  > CoPublisher AI. Be first, be right, be everywhere, on every beat you cover.
