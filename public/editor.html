Copydesk

Paste a draft. Pick a mode below. Always review before publishing — the editor can be wrong on facts.
Ask a question or tell it what to do while you write or edit — can search the web for current stats, injuries, and this week's news when a question needs it; sources cited below the reply.

Writers

Add each staff writer, then give the tool ~15 of their published pieces so it can learn their voice. Paste article text, paste article URLs, or paste the writer's 247 author-page URL and it pulls the links itself. Fully paywalled pieces may come back partial — paste those directly. ~15 is plenty; more gets trimmed.

Inserts

Reusable blocks — newsletter sign-up, VIP pitch, related-coverage boxes, ad units. Define each once (raw HTML, paste straight from your CMS) with the tier it applies to and where it lands. On the editing page the writer or editor clicks to drop them into a piece.

Podcast

Lets writers drop your latest episode into any article with one click. Pick your podcast platform, then paste the embed code your platform gives you for any one episode. The editor reuses that exact format for every other episode, so embeds look the way your CMS expects. Leave the embed code empty to use a simple audio player with a listen link.
