Help with CoPublisher AI

Contact

Email [support email]. We usually reply within one business day.

Install it on your phone

  • Android: open CoPublisher in Chrome and tap Install app (or ⋮ › Install app).
  • iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Turn on breaking-news alerts

In the app: Settings › Phone & desktop alerts › Turn on alerts on this device. On iPhone, add CoPublisher to your home screen first and open it from there. Choose which emails and alerts you get, and their days and hours, in Preferences.

Your account

Change your name, photo and alerts in Preferences. To delete your account: Preferences › Your account › Delete my account.
