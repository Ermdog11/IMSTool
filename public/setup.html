Let's set up your newsroom

A few quick questions so CoPublisher watches the right things and writes like you. Every step saves as you go, and you can change anything later.

The team, school or beat your readers come to you for.

Build your beat

CoPublisher drafts the sources that cover your beat, then you rate each one from 1 to 5. Higher-rated sources count for more when stories are rated, and a 1 stops watching that source entirely.

Rate each one: 5 must-watch · 4 high · 3 normal · 2 low · 1 don't watch

What do you cover most?

Tap each one to cycle through Core, Sometimes and Skip. Core topics get full attention, Sometimes topics only surface when they're big, and Skipped topics are always rated lowest.

Who should we watch for you?

Coaches, players, recruits, executives, alumni. CoPublisher flags any news or social media buzz about them, often before it's reported widely. Keep them in groups and rate each group from 1 to 5: news about a 5 group counts the most, and a 1 group isn't watched.

Suggestions come from AI and may be out of date. Review them before you save.
5 must-watch · 4 high · 3 normal · 2 low · 1 don't watch

What counts as breaking news for you?

Every story gets a splash rating from 1 to 5. Breaking (5) stories email your team right away with a drafted article. Pick a level for each kind of story. The defaults are a good starting point.

Teach it how you write

Paste two or three of your published articles and CoPublisher writes a house style guide from them. Every edit and draft follows it. You can edit the guide any time at /house-style.

Have a podcast?

Writers can drop your latest episode into any article with one click, in your podcast platform's own player. Skip this step if you don't have one.

Which X accounts should we follow?

Pick the X (Twitter) accounts of reporters, insiders and official team accounts on your beat. CoPublisher checks them on every scan, so you see their news first. Let us suggest some, or add your own. Skip this if you don't use X.

Suggestions come from AI. When your X connection is set up, each one is checked against X so only real accounts show.

Invite your team

Add the people who'll use CoPublisher. They get access the first time they sign in with that email. Writers can draft and edit; editors can also change settings.

You're all set

CoPublisher is now tuned to your newsroom. Your next scan uses these settings. Come back to /setup any time to change them.
