Content Editor defaults

When a writer runs a draft through the Content Editor, it can also drop extras into the story for them, like your latest podcast episode or a box of related links. Choose which ones get added and where they go. These are the starting settings for everyone in the newsroom.

  1. Switch (blue = on): turn an extra on to add it to every article, or off to leave it out.
  2. Slider: how far down the story it goes. Top (0%) puts it above the first paragraph, 50% in the middle, Bottom (100%) after the last paragraph.
  3. Save defaults at the bottom when you're done.

A writer can still switch one off or move it for a single article, right under their draft.

What each role sees and can do

Turn something off and it disappears from that role's News Monitor or Content Editor screen. Anything that spends AI credits, shows your traffic numbers or changes things for the whole newsroom is also refused by the app if they try anyway. Alerts and Settings always show. You, the publisher, always have everything.

Who can do what

What each role can do today. The publisher sets each person's role in Settings › Team & alerts.
Two more roles: Contributor (freelancers and stringers) uses the Content Editor only, sees only their own drafts and gets no alerts by default. Viewer can read the News Monitor but can't change anything or use the Content Editor. Fine-tune any role with the switches above.
